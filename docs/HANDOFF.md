Reproduce with `.venv\Scripts\python.exe src\librarian\build_curated_corpus.py`, `.venv\Scripts\python.exe src\librarian\build_curated_index.py`, then `.venv\Scripts\python.exe src\librarian\search_curated.py "your query"`. The index is versioned by a fingerprinted local file; its manifest switches only after a completed build. Runtime model loading is offline-only. Model cache and generated corpus/results/indexes are ignored by Git. Full raw-retrieval baseline and caveats: `docs/EVALUATION_FULL.md`; reranking experiment: `docs/QUALITY_AUDIT.md`.

Current preview: retrieval-only Streamlit evidence browser at `http://127.0.0.1:8766`, bound to `127.0.0.1`. The app displays excerpts, provenance, chunk IDs, and candidate routes; it only makes a course link clickable after a route is marked verified and a base URL is configured. Each result shows a role badge (book for exercise/homework, magnifier for theory/analysis, plus summary/example/general labels) and an origin. Coderslab PDFs display the local Coderslab logo at `data/Icons/codesrlab_logo_square.png`; Streamlit presentations and notebooks have separate origin labels. Labels are inferred from source path/type and should be reviewed; role labels also contribute a small query-intent reranking prior. They do not change corpus/index contents. The Streamlit process is restarted after classifier/ranking changes so fresh searches use current logic.
